# Version notes — V0.3 merged controlled release

V0.3 selectively integrates the strongest decisions from the external panel patch into the fully reproducible V0.2 architecture.

## Adopted

- Panel coverage extended to 1990-2024 using the included official World Bank workbooks.
- The core macro block is simplified to lagged GDP, population, inflation, and electricity access.
- GDP per capita remains excluded to avoid mechanical redundancy with GDP and population.
- Trade/GDP and GDP growth are retained in the source panel but excluded from the core ladder; they may be used only in declared sensitivity analyses.
- CPI remains deferred to an appendix until a raw, independently traceable source is included.
- A restoration audit now distinguishes outcome restoration from complete-case model eligibility.
- An official-versus-derived flow/GDP consistency audit is generated.

## Preserved from V0.2

- Official World Bank annual net-flow and flow/GDP outcomes.
- Signed asinh transforms that retain negative flows.
- Raw flow/GDP as a stress test only.
- No legacy stock variables in the corrected modelling panel.
- Fixed ISO3 folds, saved matched samples, five future cutoffs, four oil-rent thresholds, exact package locks, and byte-identical rerun verification.

## Not adopted

- The opaque preconstructed predictor panel.
- Raw flow/GDP as a decision-bearing target.
- Legacy stock-era variables in the authoritative panel.
- Any result not regenerated by the V0.3 root command.
