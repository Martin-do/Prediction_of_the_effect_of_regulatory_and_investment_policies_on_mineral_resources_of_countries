# Target and scope decision — V0.3

## Outcomes

- **Primary target:** annual inward FDI net inflows, balance of payments, current US dollars (`BX.KLT.DINV.CD.WD`), transformed as `asinh(US$ millions)` with negative flows retained.
- **Principal size-normalised robustness target:** signed `asinh(annual inward FDI net inflows as % of GDP)`.
- **Raw-ratio stress test:** the official untransformed annual flow/GDP percentage is retained without clipping solely to expose extreme-value and fold-leverage sensitivity. It is excluded from the final admissibility classification.
- **Forensic-only target:** the legacy inward FDI stock variable remains archived to demonstrate the effect of target misidentification and must not be used as corrected manuscript evidence.

## Resource signal

Oil rents as a percentage of GDP (`NY.GDP.PETR.RT.ZS`) are retained as the petroleum-relevant resource-dependence signal.

## Standalone scope

Aggregate inward FDI is a **national investment-context outcome**. It is not petroleum-sector, mining-sector, tar-sand, or extractive-project investment. The manuscript must therefore be framed around signal admissibility for decision support in **resource-rich economies**, not direct prediction of resource-sector capital flows.

## Prohibited claims

The paper must not claim that:

- aggregate FDI is petroleum or mining investment;
- oil rents cause investment;
- Regulatory Quality causes investment;
- subgroup results establish Nigeria-specific policy effects;
- the raw flow/GDP ratio is a stable decision target;
- a signal is admissible because one model or one time split is favourable.
