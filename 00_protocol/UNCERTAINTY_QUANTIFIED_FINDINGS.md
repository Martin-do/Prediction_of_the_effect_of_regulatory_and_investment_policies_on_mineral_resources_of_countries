# Uncertainty-Quantified Findings

The bootstrap changes the interpretation of the V0.3 point-estimate labels. Both candidate signals are classified as **setup-dependent** across the full prespecified grid. Oil rents are more stable: two setups are materially supported and three are directionally positive but marginal; five remain setup-dependent. Regulatory Quality is setup-dependent in all ten decision-bearing setups.

The result does not depend on the material category being populated. The substantive contribution is that small incremental signal value changes with target definition, oil-rent threshold, algorithm and validation geometry, even when the broader model remains benchmark-competitive.

The correct manuscript language is therefore marginal and conditional: the signals do not provide universally strong predictive increments, and positive point estimates alone do not justify direct AI–MCDM weighting.

Two oil-rent ge1pct setups meet the prespecified materiality rule, but they are explicitly down-weighted in interpretation. Their lower uncertainty bounds are close to zero, the validation environments are correlated, and the bootstrap intervals are conditional on frozen predictions rather than total refit uncertainty. They are therefore read as supportive parts of the complete setup pattern, not as independent confirmatory results.
