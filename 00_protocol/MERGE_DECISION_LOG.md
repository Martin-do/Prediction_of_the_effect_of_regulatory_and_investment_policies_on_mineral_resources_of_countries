# Merge decision log — V0.3

V0.3 is a selective integration, not a folder concatenation.

## Adopted from the external patch

- panel coverage through 2024 for the corrected annual-flow outcome;
- explicit distinction between outcome restoration and complete-case model eligibility;
- restoration self-test and restored-but-incomplete log;
- simplified core macro block using GDP, population, inflation, and electricity access;
- GDP per capita excluded from the core to avoid mechanical redundancy;
- Trade/GDP and GDP growth excluded from the core and retained only as possible sensitivity variables;
- CPI deferred to a restricted appendix until its raw source is independently available.

## Preserved from V0.2

- construction directly from included official World Bank and WGI raw sources;
- official annual net-inflow and official flow/GDP targets;
- signed `asinh` target transformations that preserve negatives;
- raw flow/GDP as a stress test only;
- no legacy stock variables in the corrected panel;
- saved Row IDs, fixed country folds, matched samples, five future cutoffs, four oil-rent thresholds, exact package locks, and deterministic rerun verification.

## Rejected

- the opaque preconstructed predictor panel as an upstream source;
- raw flow/GDP as a decision-bearing robustness target;
- legacy stock-era variables in the corrected panel;
- derived flow/GDP as a replacement for the official World Bank ratio;
- any output not regenerated by `python run_pipeline.py`.

## Additional V0.3 control

An independent flow/GDP ratio is calculated only to audit the official ratio. It is never used as a model target. The official and derived values match to floating-point precision in the included release.
