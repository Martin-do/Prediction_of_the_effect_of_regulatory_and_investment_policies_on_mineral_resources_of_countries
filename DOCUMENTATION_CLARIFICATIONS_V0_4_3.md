# Documentation clarifications — V0.4.3

1. **Construct stage.** The manuscript and Figure 1 use the label **Construct provenance and coverage audit**. The analysis verifies indicator definition, source, scale and coverage; it does not claim to perform an independent psychometric validation of the WGI latent construct.
2. **Regulatory Quality version and scale.** The frozen panel uses the Regulatory Quality governance estimate from the archived **WGI 2025 Revision** workbook. The alternative 0–100 score is used only in a labelled post hoc check.
3. **WGI temporal coverage.** The overall panel covers 1990–2024. The revised WGI historical series begins in 1996, so usable lagged Regulatory Quality observations begin after the first available governance year.
4. **Benchmark statement.** Benchmark competitiveness was generally established before the signal under test entered its corresponding model rung. For oil rents the corresponding base is M1; for Regulatory Quality it is M2, which already contains oil rents.
5. **Median interpretation.** The median base-model margin and median signal increment are separate summaries. The exact additive identity holds at the unit level, not between independently calculated medians.
6. **Scale check.** The estimate and 0–100 score produce no unit or setup verdict changes under shared bootstrap draws. This verifies representational invariance in the frozen pipeline but does not establish the construct validity or sector-specific suitability of Regulatory Quality.
