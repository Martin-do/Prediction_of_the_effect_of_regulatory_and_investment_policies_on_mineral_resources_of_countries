# Manuscript reporting assets

This directory contains the publication figures and deterministic source scripts used for the manuscript reporting layer of V0.4.3.

- `figures/Figure1_Validation_First_Architecture.*` — title-free, non-generative vector architecture at 190-mm width. Stage 2 is labelled **Construct provenance & coverage audit**, and the Regulatory Quality candidate is declared as the WGI 2025 Revision governance estimate on the standard-unit scale.
- `figures/Figure2_Admissibility_and_Threshold_Sensitivity.*` — setup-verdict heatmap and practical-threshold sensitivity curve.
- `scripts/build_figure1.py` and `scripts/build_figure2.py` — deterministic build sources.

The manuscript-facing numerical sources are generated by `04_pipeline/09_generate_manuscript_reporting.py` into `07_outputs/`, including the base-model benchmark margins, Figure 2 threshold source, and Tables 1, 3 and 4 source files.

The figures are author-created reporting assets and are licensed under CC BY 4.0 under `LICENSING.md`.
